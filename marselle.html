⚙ Breakthroughs 1976 Innovations

Game-Changing Inventions & Firsts

On April 1, 1976, Steve Jobs and Steve Wozniak founded Apple Computer Co. and unveiled the Apple I. The same year saw the debut of the VHS home video format and the very first commercial Concorde supersonic passenger flights.

The #1 Song of 1976: “Silly Love Songs”

Paul McCartney & Wings claimed Billboard’s #1 Year-End song of 1976 with the upbeat classic “Silly Love Songs,” sharing the airwaves with massive 1976 hits like Elton John & Kiki Dee’s “Don’t Go Breaking My Heart” and Queen’s “Bohemian Rhapsody.”

Touchdown on Mars & “Rocky” in Cinemas

True to Marselle’s explorer spirit, NASA’s Viking 1 and Viking 2 spacecraft made humanity’s first successful landings on Mars in 1976—sending back the first surface photos of the Red Planet while Rocky won hearts on the big screen.
